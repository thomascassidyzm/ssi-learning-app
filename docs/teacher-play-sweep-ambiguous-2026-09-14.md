## Reading the two minute columns

Own minutes are the teacher's own account on the class's course; class minutes are the Play as class account on the same course. They are shown side by side and never summed — a person's practice and a class's practice are two different things.

Where a pair failed on condition 1 or 2 alone, the sweep stopped before reading either figure, because the decision was already made; those rows show a dash.
