2 · The foldout — the thing you were unsure about

The whole player screen, captured mid-turn, scaled down and annotated with the four things the prose already names.

The whole composite is 760 wide. The phone itself is 320 of that; the rest is gutter for the labels.

The raw capture it was made from — dev deploy, so it carries a DEV badge, a reset button, a Save Progress nudge and a devtools fab that no learner ever sees. Those four are hidden in the annotated version above, because the fab sat on top of the Library button one of the callouts points at.

My call: ship the pill, park the foldout

The pill earns its place because it is the same object twice — the picture teaches the screen and the screen teaches the picture. It costs four lines of the panel and no scrolling.

The foldout is legible — but only at its own size. The phone renders at 320 across and reads fine there. The trouble is the labels: they live in gutters either side, so the whole composite needs 760, and the explainer card is 302. Drop it in as a figure and the phone squeezes to about 127 — a smudge, with callout text smaller than the prose explaining it.

So the question is not whether it works. It is where the labels go. Two ways to have it: give it a full-width surface of its own, a tappable "show me the screen" that opens it big; or redraw the callouts on top of the phone instead of beside it, which fits but crowds the screen it is explaining. I would take the first. One word either way and I will build it.

3 · The three rewrites, in full

No snippiness, and no misleading

Both rulings land in the same block. The argument against streaks is gone, the comparison to other apps is gone, and the false claim is gone.

Was: "A streak is a lovely thing right up until it ends, and then it quietly becomes a reason to stay away — which is backwards… So the app simply has no way of knowing anything about the time you spend away from it." · "A points total only ever measures the app having an opinion about you." · "Plenty of apps do this differently and plenty of people love them for it. We would just rather build the one that is glad to see you whenever you turn up."

Why there are no streaks and no points

We do not count days in a row, and we never will. The app does know when you were last here — every session is stamped with its time, which is how it picks up exactly where you left off. We simply never turn that into something you have to keep up, because pressure is the opposite of what makes this work, and we would like this to be fun.

There are no points, no score and no leaderboard either. The thing worth measuring is what you can actually say, and you can check that yourself in any conversation you fancy.

Turn up when it suits you. This is glad to see you whenever you do.

As it now reads in the app.

The honest thirty hours

Was: "Somewhere around thirty hours in the app, it turns a corner. Not finished and not fluent, but fun, easy and playful in a way the first stretch honestly is not."

What happens at around thirty hours

The first thirty hours are tough. We would rather say so than pretend otherwise. After them it really does get a lot easier — not finished and not fluent, but fun and playful in a way that first stretch honestly is not. Three things you will be able to check for yourself:

· You will be getting into conversations.
· You will be moving towards more listening work.
· You will be noticing how much surfaces easily that was genuinely impossible before.
