# What's new

**Your audio survives other apps now.** Answer a WhatsApp message, take a call, let a chime play — come back and the lesson picks up where it was, instead of going silent until you reload the app.

**Nothing gets said twice by mistake.** A phrase you'd just heard could come back at you straight away in a different voice, or a whole cycle could loop round again — that's gone, so you hear each thing when you're meant to hear it.

**Switching between Easy and Fast works mid-lesson.** Change your mind partway through and the change takes effect from the very next phrase — no reload, no leftover repeats from the mode you just left.

## Plus: bugs and stuff

- **Tapping along the phase buttons now jumps.** Tapping listen, speak, or either voice takes you straight to that part of the phrase you're on — it used to start the whole thing again from the top. You can hop around inside a phrase as much as you like.
- **The speaking bar is easier to read.** Your turn to speak is now clearly marked the whole way along, rather than looking like a blank gap, and the mic is a button like the other three.
- **Listening pods give you one new sentence at a time.** They were handing over a whole chunk of new material in one go; now each visit adds a single new sentence — two only where a line and its reply belong together.
- **Practice from earlier lessons comes back reliably.** Some lessons were arriving with none of the older material woven back in; every lesson now brings back what's due.
- **Easy keeps the early phrases short.** In Easy, the first stretch of the course sticks to shorter sentences so you get more goes at the thing you're actually learning, and the length limit lifts as you go.
