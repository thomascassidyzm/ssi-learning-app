# What's new

**Easy just got easier.** Pick Easy and every phrase now comes round twice, and the voices slow down a touch. Early on we also hold back the longer words, so you get more goes at the short stuff before things build up.

**Listening works one way now.** Every listening phrase plays the same: target, English, target, target. No more guessing which mode you're in.

…and a pile of bug fixes and speed improvements.
