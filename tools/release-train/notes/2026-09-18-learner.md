## What's new

- A review of a sentence now plays whole. Before, you could get one clip of it and then be moved on.
- The notice that a course has been updated sits under the Easy and Fast switch, where it was meant to be, instead of on top of it.
- If your own subscription has run out but you are covered by a family plan, the app now sees the family cover. Before, the old subscription could hide it.
- The app opens faster on a return visit. It stops fetching the course list again every time, and keeps more of itself ready between visits.
- Adding a second email to your account is safer. An email that belongs to an account holding any access of its own is never absorbed into another.
