# Schools note — to every teacher and school leader — DRAFT, not sent (job #195)

Drafted 2026-09-18 for the next push to main. By Tom's ruling of 2026-09-18 10:53 the in-app release notes are for learners only, and everything about schools reaches teachers through the schools inbox instead. Tom approves the words before anything is sent.

## How it is sent

- Tooling: the admin-to-learner messaging that ships on this train. One `sendAdminMessage` call per recipient, from a script shaped like `tools/admin-messages/send-own-account-teaching-note.mts`, run once after the promote has reached main. Not the composer: its audiences are one learner, a course, or everyone, and none of those is "every teacher".
- Audience: every account that holds an active teacher or school-leader tag on a school or a class, not demo and not internal. Counted live on 2026-09-18: 124 accounts. Each reads it in the schools inbox under their account menu.
- Sender: an SSi admin account. No action button attached; it is a note, not a request.
- Title: **What's new in your school dashboard**

## The note

Hello

A few things have changed in your school dashboard this week. Nothing you have set up is affected. Here is what you will notice.

**One page for each class.** My Classes is now your home page. Tap a class and everything about it is on one page: who is in it, who teaches it, the join link, the course it is on, and where it has got to. The separate class tools page has gone into that one.

**The class page shows the whole journey.** At the top of a class page is a picture of how far the class has come through its course, drawn from what it has actually practised. It leads with the class's totals, and this week's figures are the sentence beneath.

**Insights, all time.** Your Insights pages can now show all time as well as this week, and open on all time. No pupil's name appears on an Insights page.

**A Handbook, with walks.** Tap Handbook in the menu. Each thing you can do has a short entry, and most have a walk that shows you where to tap on your own screen.

**Messages from us, in the app.** This note is the first of them. You will find messages in the inbox under your account menu, and you can reply from there.

**Playing as the class.** Play as class now keeps its place properly between lessons, and a lesson played on the class is recorded as the class's, not as yours.

**Signing in, without waiting for an email.** If you are setting up a new school, you type your school email, tap once, and you are in. There is no code to wait for. The email still comes, and there is a strip at the top of your dashboard to type it into when it arrives. Until you have confirmed it, your classes cannot take pupils, and the join link tells a pupil so. If your school's mail system never lets our email through, tap Use a different address in that strip and confirm from an address that does reach you.

**Set a password.** The first thing you will see when you sign in is a place to set one. Please do. From then on you sign in with it, from any device, and school email never comes into it. If you already have an account, sign in with your password, or ask for a code if you never set one. The code screen now waits a minute before it lets you ask again, because asking again cancels the code that is on its way.

If anything here is not right for you, reply to this message and it comes straight to us.

SaySomethingin
