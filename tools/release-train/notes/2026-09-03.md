## What's new

- **The app talks to you in your own language, not just English.** Kannada, Marathi and Telugu
  interfaces are new — those were the last courses that still dropped you onto an English screen.
  And it now goes further than the buttons: "How this works", "Why this works", the six guided
  walkthroughs and the labels on the player screen itself all come through in your language,
  across the whole language list.
- **Pods handle two rough edges.** A pod that names a role now goes to the person it names,
  rather than to everyone. And a pod turn that was never recorded in the target language plays
  its known-language audio instead of falling silent on you.
- **Text in Kannada, Telugu, Marathi and other non-Latin scripts reads properly.** Where the
  design asked for italics, the browser was inventing a slanted version of scripts that simply
  do not have one. It no longer does that.

## Other stuff and bug fixes

- The Library sheet came up blank for some people — fixed.
- Signing in works from inside the app shell as well as the browser (the groundwork for the
  native app reaching your account).
- Japanese and Korean settings text that was still showing in English is now translated.
- Around 430 more on-screen phrases were pulled out of the code and translated, so future
  languages get them for free.
- The deploy watchdog no longer cries "learners can't play" when the problem is the watchdog.
- Housekeeping behind the scenes: one place that decides how the app reaches its server, one
  place that registers offline mode, and one place that answers "what am I running inside".
