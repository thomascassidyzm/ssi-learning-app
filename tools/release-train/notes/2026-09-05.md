## What's new

- A tap always lands. Tapping a belt, or jumping to where you got to, could quietly do nothing at all. Now it either takes you there or tells you it is still loading, retries, and says so plainly rather than going silent.
- You come back to where you actually got to. If you had been learning on another device, the app could drop you at the wrong point. Your real position now wins over whatever this device remembered.
- The padlock means one thing. On the belt strip it now says only whether you have access to that content, never whether you have downloaded it, and the belt panel's offline wording matches.

## Other stuff and bug fixes

- The app can now tell you which version it is running, and that a newer one is available.
- Settings shows honest build information even when the app was not built by our usual pipeline.
- The belt panel says one clear thing about what is available offline.
- Schools: a containment check was missing pupils who held a seat but no staff post — fixed.
- Payments: one place now decides what a payment button does and whether it appears at all.
- The remaining English text in the belt progress panel is now translated.
- No "add to home screen" prompt inside the native app shell, where it made no sense.
- Groundwork for the Android app: the wrapper, the store build command, and the checks that let it reach your account.
