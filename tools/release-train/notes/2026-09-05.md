## What's new

- **A tap always lands.** Tapping a belt, or jumping to where you got to, used to be able to do
  nothing at all and say nothing about it — you were left looking at a screen that had quietly
  ignored you. Now every one of those taps either takes you there or tells you it is still
  loading, and if the content is slow to arrive it retries and then says so plainly rather than
  going silent.
- **You come back to where you actually got to.** If you had been learning on another device, or
  the app had an older position saved on this one, it could drop you back at the wrong point.
  Your real position now wins over whatever this device happened to remember.
- **The padlock means one thing.** On the belt strip it now says exactly one thing — whether you
  have access to that content — and never gets confused with whether you have downloaded it. The
  belt panel's offline wording was tidied to match, so what it promises is what you get.

## Other stuff and bug fixes

- The app can now tell you which version it is running, and that a newer one is available.
- Settings shows honest build information even when the app was not built by our usual pipeline.
- Schools: a containment check was missing pupils who held a seat but no staff post — fixed.
- Payments: one place now decides what a payment button does and whether it appears at all.
- The remaining English text in the belt progress panel is now translated.
- No "add to home screen" prompt inside the native app shell, where it made no sense.
- Groundwork for the Android app: the wrapper, the store build command, and the cross-origin
  checks that let it talk to your account.
