## What's new

- "How this works" is now a proper hub in your Library — short walkthroughs of the player screen, the belts, and why the gaps between repeats keep widening, with drawings and a search across all of them.
- Every language we teach now reads properly: right-to-left scripts like Arabic and Urdu run the right way on both sides of the screen, and the app picks a font that can actually spell what it is showing you.
- Listening practice follows the new fade ladder, and it will never drop a sentence out of an exercise again.

## Other stuff and bug fixes

- National variant courses now show and keep their own flag and their own name, everywhere in the app.
- Your chosen interface language now sticks — it's applied properly when you come back, not just remembered.
- Phrases learnt and phrases spoken now count every course you have done, and they survive closing the tab.
- Total time is your measured playback time, not the clock running while you were away from the app.
- Links to saysomethingin.com open inside the app instead of throwing you out to a browser.
- Offline downloads now only bring down the listening exercises you can actually play.
- Self-hosted app fonts, for faster and more reliable loading, and a weak signal can no longer leave the app waiting on one.
