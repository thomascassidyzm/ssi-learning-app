## What's new

- SSi Family, and moving between plans without leaving the app. You can now buy SSi Family, invite the people you want on it, and add a child account that signs in with a link you send them. If you are already on Premium you can change to Family, and back again, on the subscription you already have — no second payment, no second subscription. When you change back to Premium, everyone on your family keeps their cover for thirty days, and nothing anyone has learned is ever touched.
- Buying is straightforward, and the app knows about it straight away. Choosing a plan while signed out now explains why we ask for your email, sends you a code, and puts you back on the plan you chose rather than losing your place. After you pay, the app tells you the payment has landed and waits with you until your subscription is live, instead of showing a blank screen, an endless spinner, or an Upgrade button you have already paid for.
- Speaking speed now comes from the voice that is actually speaking. Instead of one blunt speed for your belt, each clip plays at a speed worked out from the voice that recorded it, so a naturally quick voice is slowed and a naturally slow one is not dragged. Easy and Fast still mean what they meant; they are now measured against the voice in your ears.

## Other stuff and bug fixes

- Easy no longer sounds the same prompt four times in a row. You still hear it twice, and nothing is skipped.
- Settings names the plan you actually hold, rather than the one you used to be on.
- A child on a family plan is never shown a price. Every upgrade door tells them to ask their grown-up instead.
- Removing somebody from your family changes only what they can reach. A child's sign-in link stays available, and everything they have learned stays theirs.
- A family invite now says when we emailed it, and can be sent again if it has not turned up.
- An upgrade taken a day or two before your renewal is no longer refused.
- Waiting for a sign-in code: the screen now says a code can take a couple of minutes, tells you honestly when you can ask for another, and stops reading like an accusation when you have done nothing wrong.
- If somebody had typed your email address into a purchase form before you ever signed in, any way in they set up for themselves is now destroyed the moment you sign in yourself.
- The purchase screens are translated into every language the app speaks, and the German now talks to you the same informal way the rest of the app does.
