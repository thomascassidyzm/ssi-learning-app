## What's new

- You can now buy SSi Family, invite the people you want onto it, and move between Premium and Family on the subscription you already have.
- Choosing a plan while signed out now brings you back to the plan you chose, and the app tells you when your payment has landed rather than leaving you on a spinner.
- Every clip now plays at a speed worked out from the voice that recorded it, instead of one blunt speed for your whole belt.

## Other stuff and bug fixes

- Changing back to Premium leaves everyone on your family covered for thirty days, and nothing anyone has learned is touched.
- A child account signs in with a link you send them, and is never shown a price.
- Every upgrade door shown to a child tells them to ask their grown-up instead.
- The signed-out plan chooser explains why we ask for your email before it sends you a code.
- Easy no longer sounds the same prompt four times in a row — you still hear it twice, and nothing is skipped.
- Settings names the plan you actually hold, rather than the one you used to be on.
- Removing somebody from your family changes only what they can reach, and everything they have learned stays theirs.
- A family invite now says when we emailed it, and can be sent again if it has not turned up.
- An upgrade taken a day or two before your renewal is no longer refused.
- The sign-in code screen says a code can take a couple of minutes, and tells you honestly when you can ask for another.
- If somebody typed your email into a purchase form before you signed in, their way in is destroyed the moment you sign in yourself.
- The purchase screens are translated into every language the app speaks, and the German is now as informal as the rest.
