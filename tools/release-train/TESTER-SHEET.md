# The test pass — the same six steps, every time

This is the fixed sheet. It does not change from week to week, and that is the point: when the same
six things are done the same way each time, a difference between one week and the next means
something. It takes about forty minutes.

Do the whole sheet **twice**. First in a web browser on the phone, then in the app. Both against the
same address, so the only thing that changed is which one you were holding.

## The six steps

**1. Sign in as `fresh`.** Play until the first belt changes. What did you see when it changed?
Send a screenshot.

**2. Sign in as `wall`.** Play one round. You should reach a screen asking you to subscribe. Tap
**Maybe later**. Where are you now, and what does the belt say?

**3. Sign in as `premium`.** Play past the point where `wall` stopped. Pause in the middle of a
phrase and press play again. Then pause while Aran is talking and press play again. For each of the
two, tell us what you expected to happen, and then what actually happened.

**4. Open Settings.** Send a screenshot of the whole page, scrolled to the bottom as well as the
top. Tell us anything on that page that makes no sense on the phone you are holding. The very
bottom of the page says which address the app is talking to — tell us what it says.

**5. Put the app in the background for five minutes.** Do something else, then come back. Where are
you?

**6. Turn the wifi off in the middle of a round.** What happens? Then turn it back on. Does it
recover?

---

## Then the question that matters most

For each of the six steps, did the web and the app do the same thing?

**Only tell us the differences.** Where they behaved the same, say nothing — we already know where
to look. Where they behaved differently, that difference is the finding, and it is worth more to us
than everything else on this sheet.

---

## If you cannot do a step

Say so, and say why. An honest "I could not get to this" is useful and we will treat it as unfinished
until somebody does it. Please never mark something as fine because it probably is.
