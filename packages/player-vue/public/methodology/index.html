Why publish this?

Because the method is anchored in seventeen years of observation, not in secrecy — on average, learners get confidently into conversations after roughly 30 hours of practice and reach genuinely conversational ability around 100. Population averages, never individual promises. Everything above is how we're adding individual resolution to a method that already demonstrably works — rough but honest, by design, open questions included.
