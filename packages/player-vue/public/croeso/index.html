Welcome

Everyone enrolled on a Learn Welsh course can use Say Something in Welsh free for 12 months.

This pack shows you, step by step, how to get going — with a picture of every screen you will meet on the way. There is no rush, and nothing here is too hard.

The whole thing takes about ten minutes.

Before you start

  • Your phone, or your computer.
  • The email address the Canolfan’s message came to.
  • Headphones, if you have some. They are not essential.
  • Somewhere you can speak out loud. That matters more than the headphones.

Beth ydy Say Something in Welsh?

Cwrs siarad ydy o. Nid llyfr, ac nid gwers ar sgrin. Dyma sy’n digwydd, drosodd a throsodd:

  1. Rydych chi’n clywed brawddeg fer yn Saesneg.
  2. Mae bwlch tawel. Chi sy’n dweud y Gymraeg, allan yn uchel.
  3. Rydych chi’n clywed yr ateb ddwywaith, gan ddau berson gwahanol.

Dim ysgrifennu. Dim profion. Dim gwaith cartref.

Byddwch chi’n cael pethau’n anghywir, ac yn methu ambell un yn llwyr. Dyna’r bwriad. Yr ymdrech o chwilio am yr ateb sy’n gwneud i’r Gymraeg aros yn eich pen — nid y clywed.

Mae deg munud y dydd yn hen ddigon.

What is Say Something in Welsh?

It is a speaking course, not a book and not an on-screen lesson. This is what happens, over and over:

  1. You hear a short sentence in English.
  2. There is a silent gap. You say the Welsh, out loud.
  3. You hear the answer twice, from two different people.

No writing. No tests. No homework.

You will get things wrong, and miss some altogether. That is the point. The effort of reaching for the answer is what makes the Welsh stick — not the hearing of it.

Ten minutes a day is plenty.

2

Eich cyfeiriad e-bost

Teipiwch y cyfeiriad e-bost lle cawsoch chi neges y Ganolfan, ac wedyn tapiwch Send my code — ‘anfonwch fy nghod’.

Does dim cyfrinair i’w gofio, byth. Cod byr drwy e-bost sy’n eich cael chi i mewn bob tro.

Your email address

Type the email address the Canolfan’s message came to, then tap Send my code.

There is no password to remember, ever. A short code by email is what gets you in, every time.

3

Y cod chwe digid

Ewch i’ch e-bost. Bydd neges gan SaySomethingin yn cyrraedd gyda chwe rhif ynddi.

Dewch yn ôl i’r dudalen yma, teipiwch y chwe rhif yn y blwch, a thapio Continue — ‘ymlaen’.

Peidiwch â chau’r dudalen wrth fynd i edrych ar eich e-bost. Symudwch yn ôl ac ymlaen rhwng y ddau ap, ac mae’r dudalen yn aros lle mae hi.

The six-digit code

Go to your email. A message from SaySomethingin arrives with six numbers in it.

Come back to this page, type the six numbers into the box, and tap Continue.

Do not close the page while you go and look at your email. Switch between the two, and the page stays where it is.

Dydy’r cod ddim wedi cyrraedd?

  • Rhowch funud neu ddwy iddo. Fel arfer mae’n cyrraedd ar unwaith, ond nid bob tro.
  • Edrychwch yn eich ffolder sbam neu sothach. Dyma lle mae’n mynd amlaf.
  • Gwiriwch eich bod wedi teipio’r cyfeiriad e-bost yn gywir. Un llythyren goll ydy’r rheswm mwyaf cyffredin o bell ffordd.
  • Os ydy’r cod yn hen, gofynnwch am un newydd: ewch yn ôl a thapio Send my code eto. Y cod diweddaraf sy’n gweithio.
  • Os cyrhaeddodd y cod ar ddyfais arall, does dim ots — teipiwch y rhifau â llaw ar y ddyfais rydych chi’n ei defnyddio.

Code not arrived?

  • Give it a minute or two. It usually arrives at once, but not always.
  • Look in your spam or junk folder. That is where it goes most often.
  • Check you typed the email address correctly. One missing letter is by far the most common reason.
  • If the code has gone stale, ask for a new one: go back and tap Send my code again. The newest code is the one that works.
  • If the code arrived on another device, that is fine — just type the numbers in by hand on the device you are using.
4

Dau gwestiwn

Dim ond dau flwch bach sydd yma.

  • “I am aged 16 to 24” — ticiwch hwn dim ond os ydych chi rhwng 16 a 24 oed. Gadewch o’n wag os nad ydych chi. Does neb yn gofyn am eich dyddiad geni.
  • Y blwch caniatâd — rhaid ticio hwn. Mae’n dweud bod modd rhannu ffigyrau dienw am eich defnydd chi — faint o funudau, pa mor bell ydych chi wedi cyrraedd — gyda’r Ganolfan. Dydy’ch enw chi, eich cyfeiriad e-bost, na dim byd rydych chi’n ei ddweud yn cael eu rhannu.

Wedyn tapiwch Claim my free year — ‘hawlio fy mlwyddyn am ddim’.

Two questions

There are only two small boxes here.

  • “I am aged 16 to 24” — tick this only if you are between 16 and 24. Leave it blank if you are not. Nobody asks for your date of birth.
  • The consent box — this one has to be ticked. It says anonymous figures about your use — how many minutes, how far you have got — can be shared with the Canolfan. Your name, your email and anything you say are not shared.

Then tap Claim my free year.

6

Gogledd neu Dde

Mae rhestr hir o ieithoedd yn agor. Peidiwch â sgrolio drwyddi — mae ffordd gyflymach.

Yn y blwch chwilio ar y top, teipiwch Welsh. Dim ond un rhes fydd ar ôl.

Tapiwch 2 variants, ac wedyn dewiswch Northern (Gogledd) neu Southern (De).

Pa un? Yr un sydd agosaf at y Gymraeg rydych chi’n ei chlywed o’ch cwmpas, neu Gymraeg eich tiwtor. Does dim ots mawr: mae’r ddwy’n Gymraeg go iawn, byddwch chi’n deall y naill a’r llall, a gallwch chi newid unrhyw bryd.

North or South

A long list of languages opens. Do not scroll through it — there is a quicker way.

In the search box at the top, type Welsh. The list shrinks to one row.

Tap 2 variants, then choose Northern or Southern.

Which one? Whichever is closest to the Welsh you hear around you, or to your tutor’s Welsh. It does not matter greatly: both are real Welsh, you will understand either, and you can change it at any time.

Pwysig. Efallai y gwelwch chi “Premium £15/mo — Upgrade” ar y sgrin honno. Anwybyddwch o. Mae eich blwyddyn chi wedi’i thalu amdani’n barod, a does dim rhaid i chi dalu dim.

Important. You may see “Premium £15/mo — Upgrade” on that screen. Ignore it. Your year is already paid for, and you do not have to pay anything.

Eich tro cyntaf

Dyma’r sgrin y byddwch chi’n ei gweld bob tro rydych chi’n agor yr ap. Rhowch eich clustffonau am eich pen, a thapio’r botwm coch mawr yn y gwaelod.

Your first go

This is the screen you will see every time you open the app. Put your headphones on and tap the big red button at the bottom.

Beth sy’n digwydd wedyn

Byddwch chi’n clywed brawddeg fer yn Saesneg, ac yn ei gweld ar y sgrin. Wedyn daw bwlch tawel, a’r geiriau coch “YOU’RE MEANT TO BE SPEAKING NOW” — ‘eich tro chi ydy hi rŵan’. Dywedwch y Gymraeg allan yn uchel, hyd yn oed os ydych chi’n ansicr, hyd yn oed os ydych chi’n anghywir.

Wedyn byddwch chi’n clywed yr ateb ddwywaith, ac yn ei weld yn ysgrifenedig o dan y llun.

Dyna un tro, ac mae’n cymryd tua deg eiliad. Yna mae’r nesaf yn dechrau ar ei ben ei hun. Does dim rhaid i chi dapio dim byd rhwng un a’r llall.

What happens next

You hear a short sentence in English and see it on the screen. Then comes a silent gap, and the red words “YOU’RE MEANT TO BE SPEAKING NOW”. Say the Welsh out loud, even if you are unsure, even if it is wrong.

Then you hear the answer twice, and see it written under the picture.

That is one go, and it takes about ten seconds. The next one then starts on its own. You do not have to tap anything in between.

Dau beth defnyddiol

  • Easy a Fast: mae Easy yn rhoi bwlch hirach i chi feddwl. Dechreuwch gyda Easy, bob tro.
  • I stopio, tapiwch y botwm coch eto. Mae’r ap yn cofio’n union ble roeddech chi, hyd yn oed os ydych chi’n ei gau’n llwyr.

Os ydych chi’n colli ambell un, neu’n dweud dim byd o gwbl am funud — daliwch ati. Mae popeth yn dod yn ei ôl eto’n nes ymlaen. Does dim rhaid i chi ei gael o’n iawn y tro cyntaf.

Two useful things

  • Easy and Fast: Easy gives you a longer gap to think. Start with Easy, every time.
  • To stop, tap the red button again. The app remembers exactly where you were, even if you close it completely.

If you miss a few, or say nothing at all for a minute — keep going. Everything comes back round again later. You do not have to get it right first time.

If you close it and cannot find it again

This happens to everyone, and it is easy to put right.

  • Look for the red ‘S’ icon on your phone’s home screen, if you did Step 8.
  • Or open your browser and type saysomethingin.app into the address bar.
  • Usually you will still be signed in, and land straight back where you were.

If the app asks you to sign in again: tap the cog, then Sign in, give your email address, and type the six-digit code that arrives. Exactly the same way as Steps 2 and 3.

Nothing is lost. Everything you have practised is safe, and your free year with it.

Ffigyrau dienw: faint o funudau, a pha mor bell ydych chi wedi cyrraedd. Dim enwau, dim cyfeiriadau e-bost, a dim byd rydych chi’n ei ddweud.

Dw i wedi dewis y dafodiaith anghywir.

Tapiwch enw’r cwrs ar ganol y sgrin, a dewis y llall. Mae’ch cynnydd yn cael ei gadw ar wahân ar gyfer y ddwy.

Gallwch. Mewngofnodwch gyda’r un cyfeiriad e-bost ar y ffôn, y llechen a’r cyfrifiadur, ac mae’ch cynnydd yn dilyn.

Common questions

Do I have to pay?

No. It is free to you for a full 12 months. No card details are asked for, and nothing starts charging you at the end. We will write to you before the year runs out.

What if I already pay for SaySomethingin?

The screen will tell you, and show the date your free year ends. Cancelling the subscription you are paying for is yours to do — the app does not cancel it for you.

Do I need the internet?

Yes, to start, and to load new audio. The app keeps recent audio on your phone, so a short drop — a tunnel, a lift — does not stop it.

Do I need headphones?

No. The phone’s speaker is fine somewhere quiet. Headphones help somewhere noisy, and make speaking out loud feel easier.

Can I do this in the car?

Yes, and it is one of the best ways to do it. You never need to look at the screen — it is your ears and your mouth doing the work. Start it before you start the car.

I cannot hear anything.

Turn the phone’s volume up. On an iPhone, check the little switch on the side — if it shows orange, the phone is on silent. If you have wireless headphones, make sure they are connected.

I cannot keep up.

Choose Easy. Nobody is marking you, there is no clock, and there is no penalty for missing. The speaking is what counts, not the speed.

Does the app record me?

No. There is a setting called “Personalised pacing” that uses the microphone to sense when you are speaking, so it can adjust the length of the gaps. It is off unless you turn it on, and it neither stores nor sends any audio.

What exactly does the Canolfan see?

Anonymous figures: how many minutes, and how far you have got. No names, no email addresses, and nothing you say.

I have chosen the wrong dialect.

Tap the course name in the middle of the screen and pick the other one. Your progress is kept separately for each.

Can I use it on more than one device?

Yes. Sign in with the same email address on your phone, tablet and computer, and your progress follows.
