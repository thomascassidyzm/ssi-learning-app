Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 May 2026

1. Who we are

SaySomethingIn is a trading name of SaySomethingIn Cyf., a private limited company registered in Wales (company number 07234101), with its registered office at Glaslyn, Ffordd y Parc, Bangor, Gwynedd LL57 4FE, United Kingdom. SaySomethingIn Cyf. is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

2. What we collect

  • Account data: email address, display name, password (stored as a one-way hash), and any profile details you choose to provide.
  • Learning data: which courses, lessons and exercises you have started or completed, timing data, and audio responses you submit for in-app practice.
  • Subscription data: subscription status, plan, trial state. Card details and billing addresses are collected and stored by our payment processor (Paddle), not by us.
  • Technical data: IP address, device type, browser, language, and basic usage analytics needed to operate the service and diagnose issues.
  • Communications: messages you send us, including support requests.

3. Why we use it

  • To provide the language-learning service and personalise your progress (lawful basis: contract).
  • To process subscriptions and refunds via Paddle (lawful basis: contract).
  • To operate, secure and improve the service, diagnose issues, and detect abuse (lawful basis: legitimate interests).
  • To send service-related emails such as account, billing and security notices (lawful basis: contract / legitimate interests).
  • To send optional marketing emails where you have opted in (lawful basis: consent — you can withdraw at any time).
  • To comply with legal obligations such as tax record-keeping (lawful basis: legal obligation).

4. Sharing

We share personal data only with processors who help us run the service, including:

  • Paddle.com Market Limited — payment processing and Merchant of Record
  • Supabase — application database and authentication
  • Vercel — application hosting
  • Amazon Web Services — audio storage and delivery

We do not sell personal data. We will disclose data to third parties only where required by law.

5. International transfers

Some of the processors above are based outside the UK/EEA. Where data is transferred internationally, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as UK/EU adequacy decisions or Standard Contractual Clauses with additional measures.

6. Retention

We keep account and learning data for as long as your account is active, plus a reasonable period afterwards for backups, dispute resolution and legal-record purposes. You can request deletion at any time.

7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR and equivalent laws you have the right to access, rectify, delete or export your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent for any consent-based processing. You can exercise these rights by emailing us. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

8. Cookies

We use a small number of strictly necessary cookies and local-storage items needed for sign-in and to remember your preferences. We do not currently use third-party advertising cookies on the application domain.

9. Contact

For any privacy questions or requests, contact us at hello@saysomethingin.com.
