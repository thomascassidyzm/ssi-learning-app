Refund Policy

Last updated: 17 May 2026

1. Free trial

Every new Premium subscription includes a 7-day free trial. No charge is taken during the trial. If you cancel before the trial ends, you will not be charged.

2. Cancellation

You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings or by emailing us. Cancellation stops future renewals. You will retain access until the end of the current paid period; the subscription will not auto-renew after that.

3. Refunds after a paid period has started

If you are charged in error, if the service is materially unavailable for a sustained period, or if you have a statutory right to a refund (for example under UK or EU consumer rights), contact us within 14 days of the charge and we will arrange a refund through our payment processor, Paddle.

Outside those circumstances, monthly subscription fees are not generally refundable for partial periods once the renewal has been processed — but if you've forgotten to cancel and have not used the service in the new period, contact us and we will look at your situation in good faith.

4. How to request a refund

Email hello@saysomethingin.com with:

  • the email address on the account
  • the approximate date of the charge
  • a short description of why you are requesting a refund

We aim to respond within 5 working days. Approved refunds are processed by Paddle (Paddle.com Market Limited), our Merchant of Record, and typically appear within 5–10 working days depending on your card issuer.

5. Statutory rights

Nothing in this policy affects your statutory consumer rights under UK or EU law, including the right to withdraw from a distance contract within the statutory cooling-off period where applicable.
